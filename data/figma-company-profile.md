## Overview

Figma is a collaborative interface design platform used to design, prototype, and hand off digital products. Its product suite includes Figma Design, FigJam (online whiteboarding), Dev Mode (developer handoff), Figma Slides, and Figma Make (AI-assisted prototyping). The company sells primarily to product, design, and engineering teams, from startups to large enterprises, on a per-seat subscription model.
