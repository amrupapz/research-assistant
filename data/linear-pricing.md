# Pricing

Linear is free to start. Upgrade when your team needs more.

## Free — $0
For individuals and small teams getting started.
- Unlimited members
- 2 teams
- 250 issues
- Slack and GitHub integrations
- API access

## Basic — $12 per user/month, billed annually
$14 per user/month billed monthly.
For growing teams that need to move fast.
- Everything in Free
- 5 teams
- Unlimited issues
- Unlimited file uploads
- Admin roles

## Business — $18 per user/month, billed annually
$22 per user/month billed monthly.
For companies that run product development on Linear.
- Everything in Basic
- Unlimited teams
- Private teams and guests
- Linear Insights (analytics)
- Linear Asks (intake from Slack and email)
- Triage Intelligence and AI agents
- Zendesk and Intercom integrations
- Google SSO enforcement

## Enterprise — Custom pricing
Annual billing only. Contact sales.
- Everything in Business
- SAML and SCIM
- Advanced security and audit log
- Data residency (US or EU)
- Dedicated account manager and uptime SLA

## Frequently asked questions

**How does per-seat billing work?**
You're billed for each active member in your workspace. Guests on the Business and Enterprise plans are free. If you add members mid-cycle, we prorate the charge.

**Do you offer discounts?**
Yes. Startups with less than $5M in funding can apply for six months of the Business plan free. We also offer discounts for nonprofits and education.

**What changed in the September 2026 pricing update?**
Basic increased from $10 to $12 per user/month and Business increased from $16 to $18 per user/month (annual billing). Existing customers keep their current price until their next renewal after March 1, 2027.

**Can I switch plans anytime?**
Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately; downgrades apply at the end of your current billing period.
