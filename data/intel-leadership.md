# Intel Leadership

Intel's executive leadership team sets the company's strategy as it builds a world-class foundry and delivers leading products across client, data center, and AI.

## Executive Leadership Team

### Lip-Bu Tan — Chief Executive Officer
Lip-Bu Tan has served as Intel's chief executive officer and a member of the board of directors since March 2025. He has more than 20 years of semiconductor and software experience, including serving as CEO of Cadence Design Systems from 2009 to 2021, and is the founder of Walden International, a venture capital firm. He holds a bachelor's degree in physics from Nanyang University in Singapore, a master's degree in nuclear engineering from MIT, and an MBA from the University of San Francisco.

### Chief Financial Officer
Leads Intel's global finance organization, including financial planning, treasury, tax, investor relations, and capital allocation, and oversees the company's cost-reduction program announced in 2025.

### General Manager, Intel Foundry
Responsible for Intel Foundry's process technology roadmap, manufacturing operations, and external foundry customers, including the ramp of the Intel 18A and 14A process nodes.

### General Manager, Client Computing Group
Leads Intel's PC business, including Core Ultra processors for consumer and commercial laptops and desktops.

### General Manager, Data Center Group
Leads Intel's Xeon server processor business and data center AI strategy.

### Chief Technology Officer and AI Officer
Leads company-wide technology strategy and Intel's AI product roadmap across CPUs, GPUs, and accelerators.

## Board of Directors

Intel's board of directors is led by an independent chair and consists of eleven directors, ten of whom are independent.
