# Anthropic raises $22.5 billion in Series H funding

**San Francisco — October 14, 2026**

Anthropic, the AI safety and research company behind the Claude family of models, today announced that it has raised $22.5 billion in Series H funding at a post-money valuation of $410 billion. The round was led by a consortium of global institutional investors, with significant participation from existing shareholders.

The Series H is Anthropic's largest round to date. It follows a $14 billion Series G that closed in March 2026, and brings the company's total primary funding raised since its founding in 2021 to more than $64 billion.

## How the funding will be used

Anthropic plans to use the new capital in three areas:

- **Compute.** Expanding training and inference capacity across multiple cloud and hardware partners, including new dedicated data center capacity coming online in 2027.
- **Safety and research.** Growing its interpretability, alignment, and frontier red-teaming teams, which the company says will roughly double in size over the next eighteen months.
- **International expansion.** Opening new offices in São Paulo, Seoul, and Bengaluru, and expanding existing teams in London, Dublin, Tokyo, and Zurich.

## Business momentum

The company said demand from enterprise customers continued to accelerate through 2026. Anthropic now serves more than 420,000 business customers, up from roughly 300,000 at the start of the year. The number of customers spending more than $1 million annually grew more than threefold year over year.

Developer adoption has been a major driver. Usage of Claude Code, the company's agentic coding tool, grew more than 8x between January and September 2026, and the Claude Developer Platform now processes several trillion tokens per day.

## About Anthropic

Anthropic is an AI safety and research company that builds reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. The company is headquartered in San Francisco and has approximately 4,600 employees worldwide.
