# Intel Leadership

Intel's executive leadership team is executing the IDM 2.0 strategy: regaining process leadership, building Intel Foundry, and delivering leadership products for the AI era.

## Executive Leadership Team

### Pat Gelsinger — Chief Executive Officer
Pat Gelsinger is Intel's chief executive officer and a member of the board of directors. He rejoined Intel as CEO in February 2021 after serving as CEO of VMware from 2012 to 2021. Gelsinger began his career at Intel in 1979 and spent 30 years at the company, ultimately becoming its first chief technology officer and leading the development of the original 80486 processor along with 14 other microprocessor programs.

### Chief Financial Officer
Leads Intel's global finance organization, including planning, treasury, tax, and investor relations.

### General Manager, Intel Foundry
Responsible for Intel's process technology roadmap and the "five nodes in four years" plan, from Intel 7 through Intel 18A.

### General Manager, Client Computing Group
Leads Intel's PC business, including the launch of Core Ultra processors with an integrated neural processing unit (NPU) for AI PCs.

### General Manager, Data Center and AI Group
Leads Intel's Xeon server processor business and Gaudi AI accelerators.
