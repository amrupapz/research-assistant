# About Figma

Figma helps the world's product teams design, prototype, and build together, all in one place. What started in 2012 as a browser-based design tool has become a platform used by designers, developers, product managers, and marketers to go from idea to shipped product.

## Our mission

Our mission is to make design accessible to everyone. We believe the best products come from teams where everyone can see the work, contribute to it, and give feedback in real time. That belief shaped Figma's multiplayer editing from day one, and it still shapes every product we build, from Figma Design and FigJam to Dev Mode, Figma Slides, and Figma Make.

## Figma by the numbers

- **13 million+** monthly active users across more than 150 countries
- **95%** of Fortune 500 companies use Figma
- **2,140** Figmates, as of our Q3 2026 update
- **11** offices worldwide, with hubs in San Francisco, New York, London, Berlin, Tokyo, Sydney, Bengaluru, Paris, São Paulo, Toronto, and Singapore
- **2025** Became a public company, listed on the New York Stock Exchange (NYSE: FIG)

## Our people

Figma is led by co-founder and CEO Dylan Field. Our team has more than doubled since 2021, with most of that growth in engineering, AI research, and customer-facing roles. We hire across product, engineering, design, go-to-market, and operations, and roughly 40% of Figmates work in R&D.

We operate as a hub-based company: most Figmates work from one of our offices at least a few days a week, and we support remote roles where the work calls for it.

## Our values

- **Build community.** Our users are our partners; we grow with them.
- **Run as a team.** We share context generously and make decisions in the open.
- **Love your craft.** We sweat the details because our users do.
- **Play.** Some of our best ideas started as experiments that didn't have to work.
